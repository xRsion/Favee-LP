Collection and Strict, Secure Management of Personal Information

When xRsion Inc. (the Company) asks for your personal information in connection with your use of its mobile application (the App), it will always inform you of the purpose before requesting that information. We strictly manage the personal information entrusted to us and do not provide it to third parties in a form that identifies you, except with your consent or where provided for by law.

Information that identifies an individual includes information that can identify someone on its own, such as an email address, and information that can identify someone when combined with other information, such as a date of birth or name.

Personal Information Collected and Purposes of Use

Under our policy of asking you to provide only the information necessary, we may collect the following information:

Information you provide:

• Name, gender and date of birth.

• Address, telephone number and email address.

• Other information you voluntarily enter or provide.

Information collected when you use the App:

• Information about your device, such as identification IDs.

• Usage history and activity history.

• Payment information.

• Cookie information.

• Information linked with external services.

We use this information for the following purposes:

• Providing and improving the App’s services.

• User registration and identity verification.

• Preventing misuse.

• Processing payments.

• Responding to inquiries.

• Sending important notices.

• Providing information about our services.

• Analyzing usage to improve our services.

Applications for In-App Services and Payments

Your name, telephone number, email address and similar information are necessary to contact you about your use of in-app services and the processing of applications. We may also ask for payment information when you use paid services or content. Your email address is needed to send application confirmations, important notices and similar communications. Please be assured that we will not disclose or provide your email address to third parties under any circumstances.

In-App Services

When you use certain services within the App, it may be necessary to store your customer information. This is to make the services more convenient for you, and the information will not be used for other purposes.

Cookies

The App uses cookies in some areas. Cookies are small data files used to record your settings and preferences. They can identify your device but cannot identify you personally. Accepting cookies is optional, but if you reject them, some services may be less convenient to use.

Sharing Personal Information with Business Partners

We may disclose the minimum information necessary only in the following cases:

• To payment processing companies that process payments.

• To technical service providers responsible for operating and maintaining the App.

• To companies entrusted with work required to provide services.

We may also entrust the handling of personal information to third parties to the extent necessary to achieve other purposes of use. In such cases, we appropriately supervise the contractors’ security management.

Disclosure Exceptions, Correction and Other Requests

As a general rule, we do not disclose your personal information to third parties. The following are exceptions:

• Requests from legal authorities such as the police or courts.

• Where necessary to protect our rights or property.

If you request disclosure, correction, deletion or suspension of use of your own personal information, we will respond within a reasonable scope after verifying your identity.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We continuously review our handling of personal information and may change this Policy as necessary. Where a change legally requires your consent, we will obtain it in advance.

We will announce the revised Policy in the App or on our website. It will take effect when announced.
